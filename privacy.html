Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

rclone-adit is a personal tool used by a single person (the owner) to copy his own files between his own server and his own Google Drive account.

What data is accessed

With the owner's permission, the tool reads and writes files in the owner's own Google Drive in order to keep a folder of the owner's documents in sync.

How the data is used and shared

  • Files are only copied between the owner's server and the owner's Google Drive.
  • No data is sold, shared with, or disclosed to any third party.
  • No data from other Google users is ever accessed; the tool has no other users.
  • Access credentials are stored only on the owner's server, readable only by the account that runs the tool.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
